# Prompt du panorama original

Mode : outil intégré `image_gen`, nouvelle illustration. Références de direction : images fournies par l’utilisateur (Aincrad à paliers au-dessus des nuages, architecture flottante et environnement lumineux) et `ART_BIBLE.md`.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original ultrawide fantasy landscape for the Nameless website hero, 3072 × 1024, 3:1 panoramic composition, no interface in the image.
Primary request: luminous monumental Aincrad, the floating fortress of stacked architectural floors narrowing toward a tall summit. A readable stone city, tiered galleries and supported bridges belong to the same coherent architecture. The main fortress is in the right half, seen across a deep valley from a shaded stone terrace. Quiet weathered stone and a few natural branches in the left quarter provide resting space for real HTML copy. The environment has a clear foreground, a middle distance and atmospheric far mountains and clouds. The complete main silhouette and summit must fit the central vertical band, with generous sky above and space below for responsive crops. No collage, no many competing castles, no people required.
Style/medium: accomplished hand-painted fantasy environment concept art, deliberate large forms, restrained brush detail, plausible perspective and structure, clean readable silhouettes, controlled surfaces. Neither photorealism nor a generic mobile-game render.
Lighting: clear ivory late-morning daylight from upper right, blue-grey shadow on the near terrace, sunlight on the fortress and vegetation, soft distant haze. Light and depth must come from the painting itself. The right side and sky remain luminous, no blanket dark overlay.
Palette/materials: navy shadow, slate and blue-grey stone, natural restrained greens, ivory daylight, small warm old-gold architectural highlights. Matte patinated metal and cut stone. No fluorescent cyan, purple, excessive halos or glowing symbols.
Composition constraints: main point of interest between 58–78% of the width; calm low-detail left 30% for text. Keep the fortress summit away from the upper edge; preserve its complete silhouette for a wide desktop crop and a center-right mobile crop. Do not paint any text, borders, buttons, logo, watermark, map grid, UI, statistics or game HUD. No impossible dangling bridges, melted architecture, repeated ornamental clutter, deformed objects or random floating appendages.
